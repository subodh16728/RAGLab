NARUTO CHARACTERS — SOURCE DOCUMENT Scope: Naruto and Naruto: Shippuden
manga canon only Coverage: Major characters, their backgrounds,
relationships, motivations, abilities, decisions, and roles in the
story. Continuity note: This document includes major spoilers through
the end of the Naruto manga (chapter 700). It excludes anime-only filler
and Boruto-era developments. Character details are summarized in
original wording rather than copied from manga dialogue.

Naruto Uzumaki

Naruto Uzumaki is the central character of the story. He grows up in
Konohagakure without knowing that his parents were Minato Namikaze, the
Fourth Hokage, and Kushina Uzumaki. Soon after Naruto’s birth, the
Nine-Tails attacks the village. Minato and Kushina give their lives to
stop the attack, and Minato seals the Nine-Tails inside their newborn
son. The village leadership keeps the truth about Naruto’s parentage and
the circumstances of the sealing from him. Many villagers fear or resent
Naruto because they associate him with the beast that attacked them. As
a result, he spends much of his childhood isolated and desperate to be
acknowledged.

Naruto responds to rejection with loud behavior, pranks, and an openly
declared ambition: he wants to become Hokage so that the village will
recognize him. His early skills are uneven. He struggles with classroom
work and precise chakra control, but he has unusually large reserves of
chakra and a talent for persistence. After Iruka Umino acknowledges him
as a person rather than treating him as the Nine-Tails’ container,
Naruto begins to understand the importance of bonds and recognition. He
graduates from the Academy and joins Team 7 with Sasuke Uchiha and
Sakura Haruno under Kakashi Hatake.

Naruto learns the Shadow Clone Technique and later develops the Rasengan
under Jiraiya’s guidance. He also trains to use the Nine-Tails’ chakra,
although his relationship with the beast is initially hostile and
dangerous. During the time skip, Jiraiya trains him. Later Naruto learns
Sage Mode from the toads at Mount Myōboku and develops a more
cooperative relationship with Kurama, the Nine-Tails. His abilities grow
through training, experience, and the support of others rather than
through a simple desire to overpower opponents.

Naruto’s defining goal changes in depth as the story progresses. At
first he wants the Hokage title as proof that people cannot ignore him.
Over time he comes to see leadership as protecting the village and
understanding the people within it. He refuses to abandon Sasuke even
when Sasuke repeatedly rejects him, and he tries to understand enemies
whose actions are rooted in loss or hatred. His encounter with Nagato
forces him to confront the cycle of revenge directly. Naruto does not
claim to have an easy answer, but he chooses to pursue a different path
rather than continue the same cycle.

By the end of the manga, Naruto and Sasuke fight their final battle at
the Valley of the End. Both lose an arm in the confrontation, and the
fight ends with Sasuke accepting Naruto’s bond and acknowledging his own
mistakes. Naruto eventually achieves his long-held ambition and becomes
the Seventh Hokage in the epilogue.

Sasuke Uchiha

Sasuke Uchiha is Naruto’s teammate, rival, and one of the central
figures in the story. He is born into the Uchiha clan, a prominent clan
of Konohagakure known for the Sharingan. As a child, Sasuke admires his
older brother Itachi and wants recognition from his father, Fugaku. His
childhood changes when Itachi kills the members of the Uchiha clan,
sparing only Sasuke. Itachi tells Sasuke to hate him and become strong
enough to kill him. Sasuke builds his identity around revenge and
becomes emotionally distant from others.

As a member of Team 7, Sasuke develops bonds with Naruto and Sakura even
while insisting that he works alone. He is talented, disciplined, and
highly competitive. His Sharingan develops through traumatic
experiences, and he learns techniques such as the Chidori from Kakashi.
Sasuke increasingly sees Naruto’s growth as a challenge to his own
strength. After the invasion of Konoha, Orochimaru tempts Sasuke with
the promise of greater power. Sasuke eventually leaves the village to
train under Orochimaru, believing that this is the fastest way to gain
enough strength to kill Itachi.

Sasuke later defeats Orochimaru’s influence, forms a team that includes
Suigetsu, Karin, and Jūgo, and pursues Itachi. After their battle,
Sasuke learns that Itachi’s massacre was connected to a secret order
from Konoha’s leadership and that Itachi had acted to prevent a coup and
a wider conflict, while accepting the role of villain in Sasuke’s eyes.
This revelation changes Sasuke’s target from Itachi to the village and
the system he believes caused his brother’s suffering.

Sasuke obtains the Mangekyō Sharingan after Itachi’s death and later
receives the Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan by having Itachi’s eyes
transplanted. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he meets the
reanimated Itachi and learns more about his brother’s choices. He also
meets the previous Hokage and hears the history of the village from
Hashirama. Sasuke’s ideas shift again: he decides that he will become a
kind of revolutionary leader by taking the world’s hatred and becoming
its single enemy. Naruto rejects this plan because it would repeat the
cycle of isolation and hatred.

Naruto and Sasuke’s final battle ends their long conflict. Sasuke
accepts that Naruto’s bonds are not weakness and acknowledges the damage
caused by his pursuit of revenge. After the war, Sasuke leaves on a
journey of atonement and exploration. His relationship with Naruto
remains central to his eventual understanding of himself.

Sakura Haruno

Sakura Haruno is a member of Team 7. At the beginning of the story, she
is academically capable and has fine chakra control, but she has limited
combat experience and is preoccupied with her feelings for Sasuke. Her
rivalry with Ino Yamanaka began when they were younger, although the two
remain connected by friendship beneath their competition. Sakura
initially misunderstands some of Naruto’s behavior and does not fully
appreciate the loneliness he has experienced.

The early missions expose Sakura to the real danger of shinobi work.
During the Chūnin Exams and the invasion of Konoha, she recognizes how
far behind her teammates she is in combat ability. After Sasuke leaves
the village, Sakura asks Naruto to bring him back. Naruto promises to
try, and Sakura decides that she must become stronger rather than remain
someone who can only depend on others. She becomes Tsunade’s apprentice
and trains in medical ninjutsu, precise chakra control, and superhuman
strength.

Sakura becomes an accomplished medical ninja. She can diagnose injuries,
perform complex treatment, and use chakra-enhanced strength in battle.
She eventually masters the Strength of a Hundred Seal, which allows her
to store chakra over time and release it for powerful techniques and
healing. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, she helps treat large
numbers of injured shinobi and fights alongside Naruto and Sasuke
against major threats.

Sakura’s feelings for Sasuke remain complicated. She wants to save him,
but she also recognizes the harm he causes and the danger of his
choices. Her attempt to stop Sasuke herself demonstrates both her
determination and the limits of acting alone. By the end of the story,
she and Sasuke begin a family, while Sasuke’s journey of atonement keeps
him away from the village for long periods.

Kakashi Hatake

Kakashi Hatake is the jōnin leader of Team 7 and one of Konoha’s most
experienced shinobi. He is known as the Copy Ninja because the Sharingan
in his left eye allows him to observe and copy many techniques. The eye
originally belonged to his teammate Obito Uchiha, who gave it to Kakashi
during the Third Shinobi World War after Kakashi lost his own left eye.
Kakashi believed Obito had died when a mission ended in a cave-in.

Kakashi’s childhood was shaped by his father, Sakumo Hatake, a respected
shinobi who abandoned an important mission to save his teammates. The
village condemned Sakumo for this choice, and the shame contributed to
his death. Kakashi responded by treating mission rules as absolute.
During a mission with Obito and Rin Nohara, Obito challenged this belief
and insisted that abandoning comrades was worse than breaking the rules.
Obito’s apparent death and the later tragedy involving Rin deeply
affected Kakashi.

As Team 7’s teacher, Kakashi tests Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura with the
bell exercise. The lesson is that teamwork and cooperation matter more
than individual success. This lesson reflects the principle Obito taught
him. Kakashi is often late and appears relaxed, but he is observant,
strategic, and deeply affected by the loss of people close to him. He
becomes an important mentor to Naruto and later plays a major role in
the Fourth Shinobi World War.

Kakashi’s past returns when the masked enemy is revealed to be Obito.
Their conflict forces Kakashi to confront his guilt and the consequences
of the choices made during the war. After the war, Kakashi becomes the
Sixth Hokage and leads Konoha during its recovery. He later passes the
office to Naruto.

Iruka Umino

Iruka Umino is an instructor at the Ninja Academy and one of the first
people to show Naruto genuine care. Iruka’s parents were killed during
the Nine-Tails’ attack on Konoha. He understands the pain of losing
family, but he initially struggles with his feelings toward Naruto
because the Nine-Tails is sealed inside him. When Mizuki tries to
manipulate Naruto and expose the forbidden scroll incident, Iruka
protects Naruto and acknowledges him as an individual. This moment is
important because Naruto receives acceptance from an adult who knows
what the Nine-Tails did to the village. Iruka remains a supportive
mentor and a source of emotional stability for Naruto.

Minato Namikaze

Minato Namikaze is Naruto’s father and the Fourth Hokage of
Konohagakure. He is remembered as an exceptionally talented shinobi,
known for his speed and for developing the Rasengan. He was the teacher
of Kakashi, Obito, and Rin as part of a three-person team. During the
Nine-Tails’ attack on Konoha, Minato fought to protect the village and
used the Reaper Death Seal and the Eight Trigrams Sealing Style to
divide and seal the Nine-Tails’ chakra. He sealed the Yin half within
himself and the Yang half within Naruto, sacrificing his life in the
process. Minato believed that Naruto would one day be able to master the
Nine-Tails’ power and help protect the village.

Kushina Uzumaki

Kushina Uzumaki is Naruto’s mother and a member of the Uzumaki clan. She
was brought from Uzushiogakure to Konoha because of her clan’s strong
life force and sealing abilities, and she became the second jinchūriki
of the Nine-Tails. As a child she was teased for her red hair and her
ambition to become Hokage, but she later built a life in Konoha and fell
in love with Minato. During Naruto’s birth, the seal weakened and the
masked attacker extracted the Nine-Tails from her. Minato and Kushina
worked together to protect their newborn son and seal the beast inside
him. Before dying, Kushina speaks to Naruto through the chakra she left
within the seal and tells him about her life and love for him.

Jiraiya

Jiraiya is one of the Legendary Sannin, alongside Tsunade and
Orochimaru. He is a powerful shinobi, a toad sage, and a writer. He
trained Minato Namikaze and later became Naruto’s mentor. Jiraiya
teaches Naruto the Rasengan and helps him learn to use the Nine-Tails’
chakra. His travels also make him a spy and source of information about
dangerous groups, including the Akatsuki.

Jiraiya’s life is shaped by a prophecy about a student who would bring
great change to the world. He once believed that Nagato might be that
student after meeting the orphaned children Nagato, Yahiko, and Konan
during a war. He trained them in ninjutsu, hoping they could protect
themselves. Later, Jiraiya discovers that the leader of the Akatsuki is
connected to Nagato and returns to the Hidden Rain to investigate. He is
killed while gathering information about Pain’s abilities. Before dying,
he sends a coded message to Konoha. His faith in Naruto becomes a key
part of Naruto’s determination to find a path beyond revenge.

Tsunade

Tsunade is one of the Legendary Sannin and the granddaughter of
Hashirama Senju. She becomes the Fifth Hokage after the invasion of
Konoha and the death of the Third Hokage. Tsunade is an exceptional
medical ninja who helped establish the importance of medical support in
shinobi teams. Her younger brother Nawaki and her lover Dan both died in
war, and these losses caused her to lose faith in the dream of becoming
Hokage and to develop a fear of blood.

Naruto’s determination and his promise to become Hokage help Tsunade
reconsider her beliefs. She accepts the position of Fifth Hokage and
returns to Konoha. Tsunade trains Sakura in medical ninjutsu and
chakra-enhanced strength. She also uses the Strength of a Hundred Seal
and can summon Katsuyu, a giant slug whose abilities support healing and
communication. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Tsunade fights
alongside the other Kage against Madara.

Orochimaru

Orochimaru is one of the Legendary Sannin and a former Konoha shinobi.
He becomes obsessed with learning every jutsu and discovering the
secrets of immortality. The deaths of people close to him and his fear
of mortality contribute to his pursuit of forbidden techniques.
Orochimaru conducts human experiments, uses forbidden methods to
transfer his consciousness between bodies, and seeks the Sharingan and
other powerful abilities.

Orochimaru defects from Konoha after his experiments are discovered. He
becomes a major antagonist and manipulates Sasuke by offering him power
in exchange for becoming his vessel. He marks Sasuke with a Cursed Seal
and trains him, but Sasuke eventually turns against him. Orochimaru
later returns during the Fourth Shinobi World War after Sasuke revives
him. He helps Sasuke reach the previous Hokage and later participates in
the war without returning to his earlier role as the same kind of enemy.
His character illustrates how the pursuit of knowledge and immortality
can become destructive when other people are treated as experimental
material.

Itachi Uchiha

Itachi Uchiha is Sasuke’s older brother and a highly gifted member of
the Uchiha clan. He becomes a shinobi at a young age, enters the Anbu,
and is trusted with difficult responsibilities. The Uchiha clan grows
increasingly hostile toward Konoha’s leadership after the Nine-Tails
attack, partly because the village suspects the Uchiha were involved.
Some Uchiha leaders plan a coup. Itachi is placed between his clan and
the village and is recruited as an informant.

Danzo and other Konoha authorities push for a violent resolution. Itachi
accepts the mission to stop the coup and massacre the clan, but he asks
that Sasuke be spared. He leaves Sasuke alive and deliberately makes
himself appear to be a cruel murderer so that Sasuke will focus his life
on killing him. Itachi later joins the Akatsuki. His actions are driven
by a desire to prevent a larger conflict and protect Sasuke, although
the secrecy and manipulation cause Sasuke enormous suffering.

Itachi fights Sasuke and dies after their battle. After his death, Obito
tells Sasuke the hidden truth about the massacre. During the Fourth
Shinobi World War, Itachi is reanimated and meets Sasuke again. He helps
stop Kabuto’s Reanimation Jutsu and tells Sasuke that he should not have
tried to carry everything alone or manipulated Sasuke’s life. Itachi’s
story raises questions about loyalty, sacrifice, secrecy, and the harm
caused by decisions made in the name of peace.

Obito Uchiha

Obito Uchiha is a former teammate of Kakashi and Rin under Minato
Namikaze. As a young shinobi, he is optimistic, emotional, and
determined to become Hokage. During the Third Shinobi World War, he is
trapped under a collapsing rock while helping Kakashi and Rin. Believing
he is about to die, he gives Kakashi his Sharingan as a gift for
Kakashi’s promotion. Obito is rescued by Madara Uchiha, who keeps him
hidden and uses his injuries and grief to influence him.

Obito later witnesses Rin’s death at Kakashi’s hand. He does not
understand the full circumstances at first and is overwhelmed by grief.
Madara’s plan and Obito’s despair lead him to reject the real world and
pursue the Eye of the Moon Plan, which would place humanity under an
illusion through the Infinite Tsukuyomi. Obito operates under the
identity of Tobi and presents himself as Madara. He helps manipulate the
Akatsuki and eventually declares the Fourth Shinobi World War.

During the war, Obito becomes the Ten-Tails’ jinchūriki. Naruto and
Kakashi confront him and challenge his belief that the world is beyond
saving. Obito eventually recognizes the errors of his path and helps the
Allied Shinobi Forces. He sacrifices himself while protecting Naruto and
Sasuke from Kaguya. His story is closely connected to Kakashi’s guilt
and to Naruto’s belief that people can change.

Madara Uchiha

Madara Uchiha is one of the most powerful shinobi in history and a
central figure in the founding of Konohagakure. He and Hashirama Senju
were childhood friends who dreamed of ending the era in which children
were sent to die in clan wars. Their clans were enemies, but they
eventually formed an alliance and founded the Hidden Leaf Village.
Madara became the first Uchiha leader to cooperate with the Senju, but
he grew suspicious that the village would always favor the Senju. He
opposed Hashirama’s selection as First Hokage and eventually left the
village.

Madara fought Hashirama at the Valley of the End and was believed to
have died. In reality, he survived and continued developing a plan
involving the Uchiha’s stone tablet, the Rinnegan, and the Infinite
Tsukuyomi. He manipulated Obito and arranged for events to continue
after his own death. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Madara is
revived through the Reanimation Jutsu and later restored to life. He
becomes the Ten-Tails’ jinchūriki and attempts to cast the Infinite
Tsukuyomi over the world. Black Zetsu betrays him and reveals that
Madara’s plan was manipulated as part of Kaguya’s revival. Madara dies
after this revelation.

Hashirama Senju

Hashirama Senju is the co-founder of Konohagakure and the First Hokage.
He is born during the Warring States Period as the eldest son of Butsuma
Senju. After seeing children die in battles between clans, Hashirama
dreams of creating a world where children can grow up without being sent
to war. He becomes friends with Madara Uchiha before learning their clan
identities. Despite the rivalry between the Senju and Uchiha, Hashirama
continues to seek peace and eventually helps establish the Hidden Leaf
with Madara and their clans.

Hashirama possesses Wood Release, a rare ability that allows him to
combine earth and water chakra to create wood-based techniques. His
strength and healing ability make him one of the most powerful shinobi
of his era. He is able to suppress tailed beasts and helps establish the
distribution of tailed beasts among the great villages as part of the
attempt to balance power. Hashirama’s ideal of cooperation is repeatedly
challenged by the political realities of the village system and by
Madara’s distrust.

During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Hashirama is reanimated alongside
the other previous Hokage. He explains the history of Konoha’s founding
to Sasuke and fights against the revived Madara. His relationship with
Madara is central to the story’s exploration of whether friendship and
cooperation can overcome inherited hatred.

Nagato (Pain)

Nagato is an Uzumaki who grows up in the Hidden Rain during a period of
war. His parents are killed by Konoha shinobi during a chaotic
encounter, leaving him orphaned. He later meets Yahiko and Konan, and
the three become close friends. Jiraiya trains them and hopes they can
help create peace. Nagato possesses the Rinnegan, which was secretly
implanted in him by Madara when he was a child.

Yahiko forms an organization called the Akatsuki with the aim of
bringing peace to the Hidden Rain and eventually the wider world. The
group’s peaceful efforts are opposed by Hanzo and Danzo, who arrange a
trap that results in Yahiko’s death. Nagato is devastated and comes to
believe that peace can only be achieved by making people experience
overwhelming pain. He becomes the public leader known as Pain and uses
the Six Paths of Pain, bodies controlled through chakra receivers, to
fight from a distance.

Nagato leads the attack on Konoha and destroys much of the village.
Naruto confronts him after learning about Nagato’s past. Rather than
killing him in revenge, Naruto speaks about Jiraiya’s teachings and
insists that he will keep searching for a way to end hatred. Nagato is
moved by Naruto’s determination and uses his remaining strength to
revive the people killed in the attack, sacrificing his own life.

Konan

Konan is an orphan from the Hidden Rain and a childhood friend of Nagato
and Yahiko. Jiraiya trains the three children after finding them during
a war. Konan uses paper-based techniques, turning paper into weapons,
wings, clones, and traps. She helps Yahiko establish the original
Akatsuki as a group seeking peace through cooperation. After Yahiko’s
death, she remains with Nagato and supports his transformation into
Pain, even though the organization’s methods become increasingly
violent.

After Nagato’s death, Konan leaves the Akatsuki and returns to the
Hidden Rain. She protects Nagato’s body and the Rinnegan from Obito. She
fights Obito to prevent him from taking the eyes, but she is killed. Her
loyalty to Yahiko and Nagato and her original hope for peace remain
important to the history of the Akatsuki.

Gaara

Gaara is the youngest child of the Fourth Kazekage, Rasa, and the
younger brother of Temari and Kankurō. Before his birth, Shukaku, the
One-Tail, is sealed inside him. His father treats him as a weapon for
the Hidden Sand and orders attempts on his life when Gaara’s power
becomes difficult to control. Gaara grows up isolated and comes to
believe that he can live only for himself. His uncle Yashamaru’s
apparent betrayal reinforces this belief.

Gaara becomes Naruto’s opponent during the Chūnin Exams and the invasion
of Konoha. Naruto’s refusal to abandon his friends challenges Gaara’s
belief that strength means living only for oneself. After their battle,
Gaara begins to change. He later becomes the Fifth Kazekage and works to
protect the Hidden Sand. When the Akatsuki capture him and extract
Shukaku, Gaara dies, but Chiyo uses a life-transfer technique to revive
him. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Gaara becomes a commander of
the Allied Shinobi Forces and helps unite shinobi from villages that
once fought each other.

Shikamaru Nara

Shikamaru Nara is a member of Team 10 alongside Ino Yamanaka and Chōji
Akimichi, under Asuma Sarutobi. He is exceptionally intelligent and
skilled at strategy, but he initially avoids responsibility because he
considers difficult tasks troublesome. His Shadow Possession Technique
allows him to control an opponent’s movements by connecting their shadow
to his own. After the Chūnin Exams, he becomes the first member of his
class to be promoted to chūnin.

Asuma’s death during the conflict with Hidan and Kakuzu deeply affects
Shikamaru. He takes part in a plan to defeat Hidan and avenge his
teacher, but his development goes beyond revenge. Shikamaru learns that
leadership means making decisions that protect others and accepting
responsibility for the consequences. He becomes one of Naruto’s closest
advisers and a trusted strategist for Konoha.

Hinata Hyūga

Hinata Hyūga is a member of the Hyūga clan and the heiress of the clan’s
main house. She possesses the Byakugan, which gives her an almost
all-around field of vision and the ability to see chakra pathways. As a
child, Hinata is shy and lacks confidence. Her father, Hiashi, considers
her unsuited to lead the clan and places greater expectations on her
younger sister, Hanabi. Hinata admires Naruto because he continues
trying despite being mocked and rejected.

Hinata trains to become stronger and develops feelings for Naruto.
During the Chūnin Exams, she fights Neji even though she is at a
disadvantage. Later, during Pain’s attack on Konoha, she intervenes to
protect Naruto and openly expresses her feelings for him. Her actions do
not defeat Pain, but they show her willingness to risk her life for
someone she cares about. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, she fights
alongside Naruto and the Allied Shinobi Forces. Her relationship with
Naruto becomes mutual by the end of the story.

Might Guy

Might Guy is a jōnin of Konoha and the leader of Team Guy, which
includes Neji Hyūga, Rock Lee, and Tenten. He specializes in taijutsu
rather than relying on ninjutsu or genjutsu. His father, Might Duy, was
considered a failure by many because he could use only the basic
technique known as the Eight Gates. Duy nevertheless trained Guy and
taught him the value of effort and protecting the people who matter.

Guy becomes famous for his intense training, energetic personality, and
rivalry with Kakashi. He teaches Rock Lee that a shinobi who cannot use
ninjutsu or genjutsu can still become powerful through disciplined
taijutsu. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, Guy opens the Eighth Gate
of Death to fight Madara. This technique gives him extraordinary power
but is normally fatal. Naruto saves Guy from dying after the battle,
although Guy is left with permanent physical damage.

Rock Lee

Rock Lee is a member of Team Guy who cannot use ninjutsu or genjutsu
effectively. Instead of accepting that this makes him unable to become a
strong shinobi, he trains relentlessly in taijutsu under Might Guy. Lee
admires Guy and adopts his intense approach to discipline. During the
Chūnin Exams, Lee fights Gaara and reveals the speed and power he has
developed through training. Gaara severely injures him, and Lee faces
the possibility that he may never return to being a ninja. Guy supports
him, and Lee eventually undergoes surgery and continues his shinobi
career. Lee’s story challenges the idea that natural talent is the only
path to strength.

Neji Hyūga

Neji Hyūga is a member of the Hyūga clan’s branch family and a gifted
user of the Byakugan and Gentle Fist. His father, Hizashi, is Hiashi’s
twin brother. Because of the branch family’s position, Hizashi is
required to bear a curse seal that allows the main house to control the
branch family. Hizashi dies in place of Hiashi during a political crisis
involving the Hidden Cloud. Neji grows up believing that a person’s
destiny is fixed and that the branch family can never escape its
assigned role.

Naruto defeats Neji during the Chūnin Exams and challenges his belief in
destiny. Neji later learns more about his father’s choice and begins to
understand the difference between the system’s oppression and the
choices people make within it. He becomes a trusted member of Team Guy
and fights in the Fourth Shinobi World War. Neji dies while protecting
Naruto and Hinata from the Ten-Tails’ attack. His death has a major
emotional effect on Naruto and the Allied Shinobi Forces.

Killer B

Killer B is the jinchūriki of Gyūki, the Eight-Tails, and the adoptive
younger brother of the Fourth Raikage, A. He is a powerful shinobi from
Kumogakure who fights with multiple swords and has learned to cooperate
with his tailed beast. Unlike many jinchūriki who are feared or
isolated, B develops a strong partnership with Gyūki. He also expresses
himself through rap and wordplay. B trains Naruto to control the
Nine-Tails’ chakra and helps him understand that cooperation with a
tailed beast is possible. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, he fights
alongside Naruto and the Allied Shinobi Forces.

Sai

Sai is a shinobi recruited into the Foundation, a secret organization
operating under Danzo. He is trained to suppress emotional attachments
and follow orders without hesitation. Sai joins Team Kakashi during
Naruto and Sakura’s attempt to find Sasuke. At first, he struggles to
understand friendship and uses blunt language that hurts others. Through
his experiences with Naruto, Sakura, and the team, he begins to
recognize the importance of bonds. His ink-based techniques allow him to
create drawings that become real objects or creatures. Sai later becomes
an important member of Konoha’s intelligence and security work and
develops a relationship with Ino Yamanaka.

Yamato

Yamato is a Konoha shinobi whose real name is Tenzo. As a child, he was
among the subjects of Orochimaru’s experiments involving Hashirama
Senju’s cells. He survived and gained the ability to use Wood Release.
Because Wood Release can suppress tailed-beast chakra, Yamato is
assigned to help supervise Naruto when the Nine-Tails’ power becomes
difficult to control. He temporarily leads Team Kakashi after Kakashi is
unable to continue. Yamato is reserved and practical, and his abilities
make him particularly useful in missions involving Naruto’s chakra.

Kabuto Yakushi

Kabuto Yakushi begins as a seemingly helpful Konoha genin and
medical-nin, but he is secretly an intelligence operative working for
Orochimaru. He was raised at an orphanage and trained by people
connected to Konoha’s intelligence network. His identity becomes
unstable as he repeatedly changes roles and serves different masters.
After joining Orochimaru, Kabuto becomes a skilled medical ninja and
researcher. He later develops the Reanimation Jutsu to an advanced level
and uses it to revive many dead shinobi during the Fourth Shinobi World
War.

Kabuto’s conflict with Itachi and Sasuke forces him to confront his own
identity. Itachi uses Izanami to trap Kabuto in a repeating experience
until he accepts himself rather than continuing to chase an identity
built from others. Kabuto eventually helps Sasuke and the Allied Shinobi
Forces by releasing the reanimated shinobi. After the war, he returns to
the orphanage and takes responsibility for caring for children there.

Character relationships and recurring themes

Many important character relationships in Naruto are shaped by the
difference between inherited conflict and chosen bonds. Naruto and
Sasuke begin as teammates and rivals, but their relationship becomes a
struggle over whether connection can survive betrayal, grief, and
opposing ideas about peace. Sakura’s loyalty to Team 7 develops from a
childhood crush and rivalry into a commitment to protect her teammates
and become capable in her own right. Kakashi’s teaching is influenced by
Obito’s belief that comrades matter more than rules.

The story repeatedly connects teachers and students across generations.
Jiraiya trains Minato and Naruto. Minato teaches Kakashi, Obito, and
Rin. Kakashi later leads Team 7. Tsunade trains Sakura. Might Guy trains
Rock Lee and Team Guy. These relationships show how values, techniques,
and unresolved mistakes pass from one generation to the next.

Several antagonists reflect distorted versions of Naruto’s own
experiences. Gaara grows up isolated because a tailed beast is sealed
inside him. Nagato loses his family and friends during war and concludes
that pain is the only way to force peace. Obito loses faith in the world
after Rin’s death and chooses an artificial dream over reality. Naruto’s
response to these characters is not simply to defeat them; he tries to
understand the experiences that shaped them while refusing to accept the
harm they cause.

The Uchiha story is particularly complex. Sasuke, Itachi, Obito, and
Madara are connected by the clan’s history, the Sharingan, and different
responses to loss. Itachi chooses secrecy and sacrifice to prevent
conflict, but his choices devastate Sasuke. Obito turns grief into a
plan to replace reality with illusion. Madara seeks to impose peace
through the Infinite Tsukuyomi. Sasuke eventually considers ruling
through shared hatred before Naruto persuades him to choose connection
instead.
